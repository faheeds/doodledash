## Full Description (4000 chars max)

🎨 **The drawing game where skill doesn't matter — personality does!**

Doodle Dash is a fast, funny drawing party game for kids ages 8–13. Everyone gets the same prompt, 60 seconds to draw it their own way, then votes for the most creative and funniest doodles. An AI judge scores everyone too!

**HOW TO PLAY**
• Get a silly drawing prompt — draw a dragon eating pizza, a robot doing yoga, a sad sun
• You have 60 seconds — draw it YOUR way with brushes, colors, and magic stamps
• See everyone's drawings in the Reveal Parade
• Vote for Most Creative, Funniest, and Best Match
• The AI judge gives every drawing a score out of 100
• The most creative and funniest drawing WINS — not the most realistic!

**WHY KIDS LOVE IT**
• ✏️ Skill doesn't matter — a stick figure can beat a masterpiece if it's funny
• 🎯 60-second rounds keep everyone engaged
• 🎨 Magic stamps, bucket fill, 16 colors, and 6 brush sizes
• 🤖 Claude AI judges every drawing with fun, encouraging feedback
• ⚡ Earn Sparks XP and unlock new Sketchbooks with harder, funnier prompts
• 🎭 Collect Avatar Frames to show off your drawing style

**SKETCHBOOKS & LEVELS**
Progress through 3 Sketchbooks (more coming soon!):
• 📓 First Day Doodles — easy prompts to get started
• 🐾 Animal Antics — wild creatures and made-up beasts
• 🍕 Food Fight — food with serious personality

**MULTIPLAYER — PLAY WITH FRIENDS**
• Create a private room and share a 6-letter code
• 2–8 players in real time
• No strangers — room codes only, no public matchmaking
• Play on your own phones in the same room or across the country

**DAILY DOODLE**
• One fresh prompt every day, same for every player
• Climb the daily leaderboard
• Come back tomorrow for a new one!

**SAFE FOR KIDS**
• No chat — emoji reactions only
• Auto-generated usernames (DragonChef47, SunnyPenguin)
• AI content moderation on every drawing
• Report button on other players' drawings
• 30-minute cool-down for rule-breakers
• Optional parent email for safety notifications
• No ads, ever
